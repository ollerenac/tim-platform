SECURITY BULLETIN 2026-063
Severity: HIGH
Subject: Invoice-themed intrusion campaign — all indicators defanged per distribution policy

Partner CSIRTs report an ongoing intrusion campaign using invoice-themed lures against
manufacturing and wholesale trade organizations. All indicators in this bulletin are
defanged per constituent distribution policy; refang before operational use.

Initial contact arrives from the address billing[at]invoice-relay[.]example. The lure links
to a staged login page at hxxps://portal[.]invoice-relay[.]example/login which harvests
credentials and immediately relays them for interactive logon attempts.

Following access, a loader is retrieved from hxxp://update[.]kernel-patch[.]example/stage2
and tasking is fetched from the host cdn[dot]stonewall-metrics[dot]example. Interactive
operations have been observed from 192.0.2(.)203 and 198.51.100[.]23, typically within four
hours of credential capture.

RECOMMENDATIONS
Refang and block the indicators above at mail, DNS, and network boundaries. Reset
credentials for any account that submitted data to the harvesting page. Review VPN and
mail logons for the listed addresses. Report confirmed intrusions with observed tasking.
