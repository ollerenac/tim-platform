QUARTERLY THREAT LANDSCAPE REPORT Q2-2026
Sector Focus: Financial Services
Classification: TLP:CLEAR

1. QUARTER IN REVIEW
Attack volume against financial services rose 18 percent quarter over quarter, driven
primarily by credential phishing and infostealer distribution. Destructive incidents
remained rare; monetization continues to favor fraud and access resale over disruption.

2. NOTABLE CAMPAIGN
The most significant campaign of the quarter distributed a banking-focused variant of a
commodity stealer through search advertisements impersonating tax preparation software.
The installer (MD5: df5308c8768f3aff336c2356871ef24d) dropped a payload with SHA-256 c085655ce22a9ac3a4c183705c9253027581b15082c389ee51c20116ddd4476f that targeted
browser-stored credentials for regional banking portals.

Command-and-control for the campaign resolved through ledger-relay.example, with victim
data posted to https://ledger-relay.example/ingest. Interactive fraud sessions following
credential capture originated from 203.0.113.140 and 203.0.113.141, typically within one
business day of infection.

3. OUTLOOK
We expect infostealer-to-fraud pipelines to remain the dominant threat to the sector
through year end. Controls that break the pipeline early, particularly blocking execution
of unsigned installers delivered via browser download, offer the best return on effort.
Institutions should also rehearse rapid credential invalidation for customer-facing
portals, as the interval between capture and fraudulent use continues to shrink.
