CYBERSECURITY ADVISORY AA26-131B
#StopRansomware: CINDER WOLF

SUMMARY
This joint advisory details tactics, techniques, and procedures associated with the CINDER
WOLF ransomware operation, observed since January 2026 against healthcare and logistics
organizations. CINDER WOLF affiliates exfiltrate data prior to encryption and target VMware
ESXi hypervisors to maximize impact.

TECHNICAL DETAILS
Initial access is obtained through phishing messages sent from payroll-review@benefits-notice.example
containing malicious spreadsheet attachments, and through exploitation of CVE-2025-41772 in
perimeter VPN appliances. Following initial access, affiliates deploy commercial remote
management tools for persistence (T1219) and disable endpoint protection prior to encryption
(T1562.001).

Exfiltration traffic has been observed to the following addresses:
- 198.51.100.71
- 198.51.100.72
- 203.0.113.130

Data staging was performed against the following domains:
- archive-sync.example
- backup-mirror.example

The CINDER WOLF encryptor binary has the following hashes:
- SHA-1: a13a416d2246f4af8a135645bbca2f4a50a6c672
- SHA-256: 691e1ac5a347bcf3fa97ad9a60534cdd8a17c2dc22b2a793a0842daea59982b2

MITIGATIONS
* Patch CVE-2025-41772 on all internet-facing VPN appliances.
* Maintain offline, tested backups of ESXi datastores.
* Block the listed infrastructure at network boundaries and review NetFlow for prior contact.
