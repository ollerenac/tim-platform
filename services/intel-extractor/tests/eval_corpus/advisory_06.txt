ICS ADVISORY ICSA-26-160-01
Trojanized Firmware Update Targeting Building Automation Controllers

SUMMARY
An unattributed actor is distributing trojanized firmware images for a widely deployed line
of building automation controllers. The malicious firmware opens a reverse shell to actor
infrastructure while preserving normal controller operation, delaying detection.

TECHNICAL DETAILS
Victims are directed to a spoofed vendor support portal by search engine advertisements. The
spoofed portal at firmware-check.example serves a modified firmware bundle from
http://firmware-check.example/fw.bin. Once flashed, the controller opens outbound TLS
connections to 198.51.100.88 and 198.51.100.89 on port 443 and awaits tasking.

The trojanized firmware bundle has SHA-1 hash dc9e9f488e523e2eeae01166a691ad9386422891.

MITIGATIONS
* Obtain firmware exclusively from the vendor portal reached by typed URL, never via ads.
* Verify firmware digests against vendor-published values before flashing.
* Monitor controller VLANs for unexpected outbound TLS sessions.
