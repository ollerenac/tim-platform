CYBERSECURITY ADVISORY AA26-140C
Widespread Credential Phishing Campaign Delivering BrackenStealer

SUMMARY
A financially motivated actor is conducting a high-volume credential phishing campaign
impersonating invoice and billing notifications. The campaign delivers BrackenStealer, an
information stealer that harvests browser credentials and session cookies (T1555.003).

TECHNICAL DETAILS
Phishing messages are sent from the address invoice-desk@billing-alerts.example with subject
lines referencing overdue invoices. Recipients are directed to a credential harvesting page
at https://secure-login-portal.example/verify which loads additional script content from the
domain cdn-fonts-static.example. Harvested credentials are posted to 203.0.113.18 over
HTTPS on a non-standard port.

The BrackenStealer payload observed in this campaign has the following hashes:
- MD5: b5eaf234714ce8202a82ad60d93ad7fb
- SHA-256: 17fbda7935be138d65b9f9dcec096b3b76cdbab0174e3443cbb83604a1f358eb

MITIGATIONS
* Block the sender address and listed infrastructure at the mail and web gateways.
* Enforce phishing-resistant MFA so harvested passwords alone cannot be replayed.
* Educate finance teams on invoice-themed lures during quarter close.
