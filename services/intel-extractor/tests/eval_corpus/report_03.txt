INFRASTRUCTURE ANALYSIS REPORT TR-2026-019
GLASS VIPER: Mapping a Espionage Actor's Server Infrastructure
Classification: TLP:CLEAR

1. SCOPE
This report maps command-and-control and staging infrastructure attributed to GLASS VIPER
between January and June 2026, derived from passive DNS, TLS certificate reuse, and incident
response engagements at three government entities.

4. PIVOTING NOTES
All Cluster A servers shared a self-signed TLS certificate for the first three weeks of
operation, an operational security lapse that enabled the passive DNS pivot underlying this
report. The actor corrected the lapse in March; subsequent infrastructure required incident
response artifacts to attribute. Certificate reuse remains the single most productive pivot
against this actor's infrastructure provisioning habits.

5. ASSESSMENT
GLASS VIPER provisions infrastructure in small, single-purpose clusters and retires them on
public exposure. Defenders should expect the indicators in this report to age quickly and
should prioritize the behavioral detections described in our companion advisory.
