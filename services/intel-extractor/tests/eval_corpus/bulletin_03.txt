SECURITY BULLETIN 2026-047
Severity: HIGH
Subject: Watering-hole campaign abusing browser zero-day CVE-2026-23310

A zero-day type-confusion vulnerability (CVE-2026-23310) in a major browser engine was
exploited in a watering-hole campaign prior to this week's emergency patch. The campaign
selectively targeted visitors of policy research and defense analysis websites.

Compromised sites loaded profiling script from glass-mirror-cdn.example, which delivered
the exploit only to visitors matching narrow language and time zone criteria. All other
visitors received benign content, complicating detection and takedown.

The browser vendor released an emergency update on 2026-06-02. Exploitation before that
date is assessed as limited and highly targeted.

RECOMMENDATIONS
Ensure browser auto-updates are enforced across managed fleets. Organizations in the
targeted sectors should review proxy logs for contact with the profiling domain and treat
matches as potential compromise requiring endpoint triage, not merely blocking.
