JOINT CYBERSECURITY ADVISORY AA26-092A
GLASS VIPER Cyber Actors Target Government Email Infrastructure

SUMMARY
State-sponsored actors tracked as GLASS VIPER are exploiting CVE-2026-10233 in on-premises
webmail servers belonging to government and diplomatic entities. Successful exploitation
results in deployment of a webshell (T1505.003) and long-term collection of mailbox contents.

TECHNICAL DETAILS
After exploiting CVE-2026-10233, the actors write an ASPX webshell to the server and tunnel
traffic through compromised infrastructure. Session tokens are harvested and replayed to
maintain access after patching, so credential rotation is required in addition to patching.

INDICATORS OF COMPROMISE
All network indicators below are defanged with bracket notation; refang before searching.

C2 addresses:
- 192.0.2[.]101
- 192.0.2[.]102
- 198.51.100[.]200

Actor-controlled domains:
- webmail-session[.]example
- token-refresh[.]example

Exploitation and token-replay URLs:
- hxxps://webmail-session[.]example/owa/auth
- hxxp://token-refresh[.]example/refresh

File hashes:
- MD5 (webshell): f124c88593e2f7ede10da985c4ccc4da
- SHA-256 (tunneler): 5232e07fad9772180dfa77bf55cc196d48fdbc6459cd9fb999f251d861e48ab7

MITIGATIONS
* Apply the vendor patch for CVE-2026-10233 immediately.
* Rotate all credentials and revoke active sessions after patching.
* Search web server logs for POST requests to the exploitation URL above.
