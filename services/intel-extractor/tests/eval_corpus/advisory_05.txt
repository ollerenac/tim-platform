CYBERSECURITY ADVISORY AA26-155A
HOLLOW CRANE Botnet Targeting Small Office Routers

SUMMARY
The HOLLOW CRANE botnet is compromising end-of-life small office and home office routers via
default credentials and a chain of known vulnerabilities. Compromised devices are used as
residential proxies to obfuscate follow-on intrusions against enterprise targets.

TECHNICAL DETAILS
Infected routers beacon to a tiered C2 architecture and periodically retrieve updated bot
binaries. Two payload variants have been observed, differing only in persistence mechanism.
Operators rotate relay infrastructure weekly; the addresses below were active in May 2026.

MITIGATIONS
* Replace end-of-life routers that no longer receive firmware updates.
* Reboot suspected devices and change administrative credentials from a trusted network.
* Block the listed infrastructure and monitor for beaconing on unusual high ports.
