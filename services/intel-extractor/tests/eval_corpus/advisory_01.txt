CYBERSECURITY ADVISORY AA26-118A
Release Date: April 28, 2026

VELVET MANTIS Actors Exploiting Internet-Exposed HMI Interfaces at Water and Wastewater Facilities

SUMMARY
The Cybersecurity Directorate is releasing this advisory to warn of ongoing activity by the
actor tracked as VELVET MANTIS targeting human-machine interface (HMI) software at water and
wastewater treatment facilities. Since February 2026, the actors have exploited weak
credentials on internet-exposed HMIs to modify pump telemetry set points and to deploy the
StoneLoader implant.

TECHNICAL DETAILS
VELVET MANTIS actors authenticate to exposed HMI panels using default vendor credentials,
consistent with MITRE ATT&CK technique T1078 (Valid Accounts). After initial access, the
actors download a second-stage payload from attacker-controlled infrastructure and establish
persistence via a scheduled task (T1053.005).

Observed command-and-control infrastructure:
- 192.0.2.44
- 192.0.2.45
- 198.51.100.12
- 203.0.113.9

Observed staging domains:
- pump-telemetry.example
- scada-sync.example

Payload retrieval URLs:
- http://pump-telemetry.example/init
- https://scada-sync.example/cfg

File hashes associated with StoneLoader:
- MD5: 75668ad1bb3915fca2209c165b7afc5c
- SHA-256: 4c7bf192284983cdb53f47f5f059861bd601e68a900004c7c2d5b1edcf9462ac

MITIGATIONS
* Change default HMI credentials immediately and enforce phishing-resistant MFA.
* Disconnect HMI panels from the public internet or place them behind a VPN.
* Hunt for the indicators above in perimeter and DNS logs going back to February 2026.
* Report intrusions to your national CSIRT.

The indicators listed are attributable to activity observed between February and April 2026
at multiple water sector entities. This advisory is provided for informational purposes.
