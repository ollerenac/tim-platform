THREAT INTELLIGENCE REPORT TR-2026-014
Campaign Analysis: VELVET MANTIS Operations Against North American Water Utilities
Classification: TLP:CLEAR

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Between February and May 2026, the intrusion set tracked as VELVET MANTIS conducted a
sustained campaign against water and wastewater utilities in North America. The campaign
combined opportunistic exploitation of internet-exposed human-machine interfaces with a
purpose-built implant, StoneLoader, and demonstrated a working knowledge of industrial
control processes. We assess with moderate confidence that the objective was pre-positioning
rather than immediate disruption.

2. CAMPAIGN TIMELINE
February: initial access activity against HMI panels using vendor default credentials.
March: deployment of StoneLoader at six utilities; telemetry set points modified at two.
April: infrastructure rotation following public reporting; tempo reduced but not halted.
May: renewed activity with an updated implant variant and new staging infrastructure.

3. TECHNICAL FINDINGS
Initial access relied on valid accounts (T1078) against exposed panels. Persistence used
scheduled tasks (T1053.005). The implant retrieved tasking over HTTPS with a beacon interval
derived from the compromised host clock, complicating time-based correlation. Lateral
movement inside OT networks was limited and deliberate, suggesting an emphasis on stealth.
