SECURITY BULLETIN 2026-058
Severity: MEDIUM
Subject: Phishing campaign impersonating research grant administration

A sustained phishing campaign is targeting universities and research institutes with
messages impersonating grant administration correspondence. Messages are sent from
grants-office@funding-portal.example and reference plausible, publicly listed grant
program identifiers to increase credibility.

Recipients are asked to confirm banking details for disbursement via an attached form. The
form submits entered data to actor infrastructure. Finance and research administration
staff are the primary targets; several institutions report near-miss fraudulent transfers.

RECOMMENDATIONS
Block the sender address at the mail gateway and alert finance staff to the lure theme.
Verify any change of banking details through a known-good channel before processing.
Report incidents to your sector CSIRT, including message headers where available.
