INCIDENT REPORT IR-2026-0102
Compromised Software Update Channel at a Regional MSP
Classification: TLP:CLEAR (sanitized)

1. SUMMARY
A regional managed service provider's software update channel was compromised for eleven
days in May 2026, resulting in the distribution of a backdoored maintenance agent to 214
downstream customer endpoints. This report sanitizes and shares the technical findings.

2. FINDINGS
The actor obtained access to the MSP's build server through a reused administrator password
exposed in an unrelated breach. A modified update package (MD5: 7f969b028baa123f4296a39d0378836b) was inserted
into the update channel; the embedded backdoor (SHA-256: 1e6846efdd186fa786b2c8df624ca118d8a920756f24cf3796a565d5c7b4e5b4) established
persistence and beaconed to two addresses:
  198.51.100.111
  198.51.100.112

Beacon traffic used the domain agent-heartbeat.example for its TLS SNI value while
connecting directly to the addresses above, a mismatch that ultimately enabled network
detection. Secondary payloads were staged at update-manifest.example and retrieved from
https://update-manifest.example/pkg/agent by hosts selected for follow-on activity.

3. DOWNSTREAM IMPACT
Of 214 endpoints that received the package, 31 exhibited follow-on interactive activity.
Affected customers were notified within 72 hours of confirmation.

4. RECOMMENDATIONS
Build infrastructure deserves production-grade access controls: unique credentials,
hardware-backed MFA, and egress restriction. Downstream customers should verify update
signatures where the mechanism exists and alert on SNI-to-destination mismatches.
